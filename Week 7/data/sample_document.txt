Welcome to Acme Corporation. This handbook summarizes the key policies every employee
should know. It is a sample document so you can see the RAG pipeline working end-to-end.

WORK HOURS
The standard work week is Monday through Friday, 9:00 AM to 6:00 PM, with a one-hour
lunch break. Flexible hours are available with manager approval, but every employee must
be reachable between 10:00 AM and 4:00 PM core hours. Employees may work from home up to
two days per week after completing their probation period.

LEAVE POLICY
Every full-time employee earns 24 paid leave days per year, in addition to 10 public
holidays. Sick leave is separate and does not reduce annual leave. To request leave,
submit a request through the HR portal at least three working days in advance for planned
leave, or on the same day for emergencies. Leave can be carried forward to the next
calendar year up to a maximum of 10 days.

HEALTH AND INSURANCE
Acme provides comprehensive health insurance to all employees and their dependents from
the first day of employment. The policy covers outpatient care, hospitalization, dental,
and vision. Preventive check-ups are fully covered once a year. A wellness allowance of
$500 per year can be reimbursed for gym memberships, sports equipment, or health apps.

PROBATION AND REVIEWS
New employees complete a probation period of six months. During probation, performance
is reviewed at the third month and at the end of the period. After probation, all
employees have a formal performance review every six months. Promotions and salary
revisions are linked to these review outcomes.

CONFIDENTIALITY AND INTELLECTUAL PROPERTY
Employees must keep company information confidential both during and after employment.
All work produced during employment, including code, documents, and designs, is the sole
property of Acme Corporation. Using company resources for personal commercial projects is
not permitted without written approval from the legal team.

TRAINING AND DEVELOPMENT
Acme reimburses up to $1,000 per year for approved training courses, certifications, and
conference tickets. Employees are encouraged to spend up to four hours per week during
work hours on learning new skills relevant to their role. Paid study leave of up to five
days per year is available for certification exams.
